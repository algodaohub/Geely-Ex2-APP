# iOS Installation Guide (iPhone)

Install file: [⬇️ GeelyEX2.ipa](GeelyEX2.ipa)

## Requirements

- iPhone/iPad running iOS 12 or later.
- A Mac or Windows computer with **Sideloadly** installed.
- An Apple ID (a free Apple ID works fine).

## Installation Steps

1. Download and install **Sideloadly** from: https://sideloadly.io
2. Plug your iPhone into the computer with a cable (an original cable is recommended).
3. On the iPhone, tap **Trust** if you see the **Trust This Computer** prompt.
4. Open **Sideloadly**.
5. Drag the [⬇️ `GeelyEX2.ipa`](GeelyEX2.ipa) file into the Sideloadly window.
6. Select your iPhone in the **Device** box.
7. Enter the **Apple ID** (and password) of the person who will use the app in the **Apple ID** box.
8. Click **Start** and wait for the signing + installation process to finish.
9. On the iPhone, go to:
   **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**
10. Tap the developer profile of the Apple ID you used, then select **Trust**.
11. Go back to the home screen and open the **Geely EX2** app.

## Important Notes

- With a free Apple ID, the app **expires after about 7 days**. When it expires, plug the phone back into the computer and re-sign/reinstall with Sideloadly (your app data is kept — it only needs to be re-signed).
- When installing for someone else, use **their own Apple ID** to sign in Sideloadly, so they don't have to refresh periodically.
- Don't delete the app after installing if it uses WiFi/Local Network features.

## Uninstall

Press and hold the app icon on the home screen > **Remove App** > **Delete**.
