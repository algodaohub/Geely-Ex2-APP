## 📱 Installation Guides

> 👉 **Jump right in:**

- [**Install on iOS (iPhone)**](INSTALL-IOS.en.md)
- [**Install on Android (phone + head unit)**](INSTALL-ANDROID.en.md)

### Tips

- Installing the app on your phone (iOS or Android) is simple — just follow the matching guide.
- Installing the app on the **head unit** requires unlocking ADB first — follow Part 2 of the Android guide carefully and read it fully before you start.
